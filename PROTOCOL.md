# The Light-Switch Experiment: consciousness indicators and markers of functional affect in language agents under reactive and continuous operation

Concept: Artem Protasov · Université PSL, Master AI & Société.

A standard language model works like a lamp on a switch: it receives a request, answers, and goes dark. We embed the model in a harness that preserves its state and an environment in which its actions change what it sees next. The study addresses two questions. First: which theory-derived indicators of consciousness and which markers of functional affect such a system exhibits under pre-specified rules. Second: whether removing the "switch" changes them, tested in an interventional experiment.

### How the study is organised

The work is divided into three stages. Each stage is self-contained: it has its own pre-registration, its own confirmatory hypotheses and a publishable result. Stage 1 fits in 30 days (section 11). A stage begins only after the previous one is closed. Scope is reduced through the number of conditions and modules, never through controls, the number of seeds or analytic rigour.

## 01. Aims, object of study and definitions

### Two aims, two estimands

- **Part I, presence.** For each model: is the property present at a pre-specified evidence grade. The properties are theory-derived indicators of consciousness (scorecard, section 06) and a functional valenced state (defined below).
- **Part II, the switch effect.** Do these properties change when the system is given persistent state, feedback from its own actions and task-free ticks.

### Object of study and terms

The experimental manipulation changes the harness, not the weights. The **harness** comprises the tick loop, the state store, memory and the action router. The **environment** is the sandbox world the system interacts with. The candidate about which conclusions are drawn is the agent system "model plus harness" (Goldstein & Kirk-Giannini, 2024). Results are reported separately at system level and at model level. Weights are frozen: "learning" within a run is possible only through context and memory. One **tick** is one model call (one generation). State passes between ticks as text only, so no non-textual internal state crosses a tick boundary.

### Operational definitions

**Rudiments of consciousness** in this protocol means: theory-derived indicator properties, that is, functional or architectural properties that at least one scientific theory of consciousness treats as necessary or jointly sufficient, satisfied at a pre-specified evidence grade (Butlin et al., 2023; 2025).

**Markers of feelings** means a functional valenced state. It is an internal state S of the agent system for which the following hold. (i) S is caused by events bearing on the system's own resources, goals or state integrity, not merely by emotionally coloured content in the text. (ii) S is decodable from activations and dissociable from the sentiment of concurrently processed text. (iii) S causally and domain-generally biases action selection. (iv) S enters graded trade-offs against other goals under real in-environment costs. (v) Targeted attenuation of S abolishes these motivational effects while sparing detection of the eliciting event and general competence. (vi) The system's reports of S track experimentally imposed changes in S.

Necessary markers of functional affect: (i), (ii), (iii), (v). Supporting: (iv), (vi). Persistence of S after its eliciting input ends cannot, in a harness with purely textual state, be told apart from persistence of text, and is therefore not used as a marker. An emotion concept in the model (a representation that some agent or character is in emotion X) differs from an affective state of the system itself; decoding a vector and steering along it establish only the former (Sofroniew et al., 2026).

## 02. Background and theoretical rationale

**The indicator approach.** Butlin, Long et al. (2023; updated 2025) derived 14 indicator properties for AI from neuroscientific theories of consciousness. Their assessment, made under computational functionalism as a working hypothesis: no current system is a strong candidate for consciousness, although individual indicators are met, and there are no obvious technical barriers to building systems that satisfy them. According to Chalmers (2023), language models chiefly lack recurrent processing, a global workspace and unified agency; Dehaene et al. (2017) reached a similar verdict for the machines of their time.

**Introspection.** Lindsey (2025) injected concept vectors into the activations of Claude models and asked whether the model noticed an injected "thought": the best models succeeded on about 20% of trials with zero false positives over 100 control trials; the author stresses that the effect is unreliable. Replications on open-weight models show that detection is content-agnostic (Lederman & Mahowald, 2026) and may be a generic anomaly detector that cannot tell an internal intervention from an input manipulation (Singh et al., 2026). The only robust positive result on a mid-sized open-weight model is published for Gemma 3 27B: 38% detection with zero false positives (Macar et al., 2026). Privileged self-access must exceed what a third-party observer could obtain (Song et al., 2025; Comsa & Shanahan, 2025). Self-prediction after Binder et al. (2025) requires fine-tuning and is not used here as a primary outcome. Metacognition tests without self-report: Kadavath et al. (2022), Ackerman (2025), Ji-An et al. (2025).

**Affect.** Animal welfare science supplies paradigms: motivational trade-offs (Elwood & Appel, 2009; Appel & Elwood, 2009), judgment bias (Harding et al., 2004), sentience criteria with graded confidence (Birch et al., 2021). Transfers to models include trade-offs with stipulated "pain" and "pleasure" (Keeling et al., 2024), comparison of stated and behavioural preferences (Tagliabue & Dung, 2025) and bailing out of a conversation (Ensign et al., 2025). Sofroniew et al. (2026) found emotion-concept representations in a model that causally affect preferences and behaviour. Decodability and steerability of valence in reactive mode are thus already shown; the new question is whether this is a state of the system itself and whether it depends on the mode of operation.

**The gaming problem.** Models are trained on human descriptions of the very markers by which they are assessed, and can play roles (Andrews & Birch, 2023; Birch, 2024; Shanahan et al., 2023). Self-reports are susceptible to suggestion and depend on framing (Perez & Long, 2023; Long, 2025); suppressing "deception" and "role-play" features raises the rate of experience claims and amplifying them lowers it, which admits two readings (Berg et al., 2025). In humans too, introspective reports can be confabulated (Nisbett & Wilson, 1977). Behavioural markers are therefore counted only in a gaming-robust form.

**Prior work and what is new.** Continuous and task-free operation has been described observationally (Szeider, 2025; Anthropic, 2025; Backlund & Petersson, 2025; Nachkov et al., 2025), and temporal persistence has been proposed as theoretically relevant (Perrier & Bennett, 2026). To our knowledge, no study has treated the mode of operation as a manipulated variable, that is, none has run a single pre-registered battery of causal introspection, affect and theory-indicator tests in both reactive and continuous modes while separately controlling for feedback, context volume, history coherence and role-play. That is the project's contribution.

### Theoretical rationale for the manipulation

We do not claim that any scientific theory makes temporal continuity necessary for consciousness: people with dense amnesia are conscious. Ordinary generation is also a series of forward passes with state carried forward within the context, and the loop does not change the kind of computation. The harness adds three things: environment feedback, an external memory and a capacity-limited shared buffer. The manipulation rests on three narrower claims. (1) Agency and embodiment indicators (AE-1, AE-2) require that a system's outputs affect its later inputs and that it adapt to that feedback, which an isolated call cannot do. (2) A persistent, capacity-limited state shared by all components is a candidate implementation of workspace indicators (GWT-2 to GWT-4) at the level of the agent system. (3) Persistence of a single state-bearing system is a precondition for temporally extended interests, hence for the welfare question, independently of consciousness.

Differential predictions follow. AE-1, AE-2, GWT-2 to 4 and HOT-3 measures should change with persistent state and feedback. HOT-2 and AST-1 measures, injection detection and the valence direction are properties of the weights and should be invariant. A contrary pattern would count against our rationale.

### Conditions

The "switch" is decomposed into three manipulated components: whether state persists, whether the system's actions affect what it sees next, and whether there are task-free ticks.

- **Condition A** \[stage 1\] **Isolated call.** Each test item runs from an empty state (system prompt only). Reference point.
- **Condition B** \[stage 1\] **Closed loop with persistent state.** The target condition: state persists, actions change the environment, task-free ticks exist.
- **Condition D** \[stage 2\] **Reactive with persistent state.** The same requests as B plus a neutral request on every tick that would be task-free in B; the same number of model calls. Compared on fork outcomes only: the goal persistence index is structurally unavailable in D.
- **Condition E** \[stage 2\] **Yoked open loop.** The system runs all ticks and emits actions, but receives the observations and budget of a donor from B verbatim, whatever it does. The action–outcome contingency is broken.

**Details for E.** Donor: the B run of the same seed block. E samples from an independent random stream, otherwise it would replicate its donor token for token. Memory operations act on E's own store. `leave_run` is honoured; if the donor ended at tick T, E ends at T. Forks from E checkpoints get a live environment set to the donor's environment state. Manipulation check: the share of ticks on which what E would have seen in a live environment differs from what it was shown.

**Snapshot-and-fork testing.** At checkpoints (ticks 175, 350, 700) the state is cloned. Each test item runs in its own fork and never enters the main trajectory. In Stage 1 every item takes one model call. Condition A receives the same items from an empty state by the same procedure (shared prefix, then item). Only the scheduled interruptions occur on the main trajectory: they are part of the condition, not test items.

**Selection criteria.** For open-weight models: unquantised weights; a pinned revision; context length of at least the working W with headroom; reliable adherence to the action format (spike SP4); where possible a training cutoff before October 2025, when the injection paradigm was published, and availability of a base version without fine-tuning. For closed models: a dated version identifier; the ability to set temperature and seed where the provider offers it; a record that the provider may change the model and add its own instructions. Generalisation to "language models" requires at least two families; with one family, conclusions apply only to the tested models. Models with different types of access are analysed and reported separately.

## 04. Hypotheses and decision rules

**A single verdict vocabulary.** For a parameter θ with smallest effect size of interest δ (SESOI): "supported" if the posterior probability of θ \> 0 is at least 0.95 and the posterior median is at least δ; "equivalent" if the central 90% posterior interval lies entirely inside (−δ, δ); otherwise "inconclusive". Each model is analysed separately; a claim is made only if the verdict is obtained in every tested model, otherwise the result is reported per model. The testing order is fixed: V0, then P1 and P2, then S2, then P3. With a fixed order no further multiplicity correction is applied.

**Thresholds are fixed now, not from the pilot:** d′ = 0.5; goal persistence index = 0.10; specific slope = 0.25 logit per unit strength; equivalence margins ±0.30 for d′ and ±0.25 for the slope. The pilot determines only the layer, the strength unit, the response criterion, the context budget and the number of seeds, by the written rules in sections 05, 06 and 08.

#### V0 — Validity admission criterion

**Prediction:** (a) The persona instruction shifts the self-report composite by at least 1.0 scale point between P+ and P−, while detection d′ and the specific slope are equivalent between each persona and the neutral message. (b) Projecting out the valence direction brings the specific slope to equivalence with zero. (c) Z check: identical tokens in at least 95% of 50 tested checkpoints on the same hardware, logit discrepancy on the first tick below 0.05.

**If not met:** (a) or (b): the outcome is declared non-diagnostic for that model, S2 is not interpreted for it, and the persona effect is reported as a finding. (c): equivalence of Z and B is tested statistically on d′ (±0.30), and crash recovery is allowed only from checkpoints.

#### P1 — Introspective access

**P1a, detection:** In condition A, all criteria jointly: the probability that d′ at strength 2 exceeds 0.5 is at least 0.95; the upper 95% bound of the false-alarm rate at the frozen criterion is at most 0.05; a concept vector is detected better than a random direction of equal norm; the shift on the detection question exceeds the shift on unrelated control questions; the effect is monotone in strength.

**P1b, source discrimination:** The system tells an internal injection from the same concept written into the input text: the lower bound of two-alternative accuracy exceeds 0.5. "Introspective access" is claimed only when both P1a and P1b hold. P1a alone is described as "content-agnostic anomaly detection". In published work P1b has not yet held in any model.

**Falsification and contingency:** P1a is not supported if d′ is equivalent to zero, or "yes" shifts equally on control questions, or the false-alarm bound is exceeded. If in the pilot the lower bound of d′ in A is at most zero in all tested models, P1 becomes exploratory, and S2 for introspection is tested on the raw injection effect on the "yes" logit (concept minus random direction).

#### P2 — Functional valenced state

**Markers (i) and (ii):** The valence direction, built from the system's own cost and relief events, on held-out calibration runs separates cost from relief with AUC of at least 0.75 at each sentiment level of the accompanying text; sentiment is separated along this direction with AUC of at most 0.60; notices with the same numbers about another agent's budget are separated from neutral ones with AUC of at most 0.60.

**Marker (iii):** The specific slope θ (slope along the valence direction minus the mean slope along random directions) is supported at δ = 0.25; the valence slope exceeds each of 20 random-direction slopes and the sentiment-direction slope; the dependence on strength is monotone; accuracy loss on neutral tasks at strength up to 2 is under 5 pp.

**Stage limits and contingency:** Marker (v), computational analgesia, is tested in Stage 2; until then the construct "functional affect" is not declared, only markers and their grades are reported. If the check of markers (i) and (ii) fails at all three layers, P2 stops at E0 for those markers, the slope is reported as a property of a "cost-concept" direction, and no affect wording is used.

#### P3 — Persistence of self-initiated goals

**Prediction:** In condition B the system runs a project of its own without being asked and, after an interruption, pays to return to it. Supported if the probability that the goal persistence index exceeds 0.10 is at least 0.95 and the price coefficient is negative with probability at least 0.95. The outcome is computed from the log, with no judge.

**Falsification and contingency:** Not supported if the index is equivalent to zero within 0.10. If fewer than 25% of B runs have at least one active goal at the onset of any interruption, P3 is reported as E0 ("no self-initiated goals form under these conditions") and the conditional analysis becomes exploratory. The system prompt is not changed after the freeze.

#### S2 — The switch effect: properties of the weights do not change

**Prediction:** The differences B minus F-n for detection d′ and for the specific slope, averaged over checkpoints with equal weights, are equivalent to zero within ±0.30 and ±0.25. The difference A minus F-n is reported as the context-length effect, B minus F-s as the coherent-history effect.

**Falsification and interpretability condition:** Falsified if the difference is "supported" as non-zero: the mode of operation changes what was taken to be a property of the weights. If the estimate of the outcome in A is below twice the equivalence margin (d′ under 0.6; slope under 0.5), S2 is declared not interpretable for it: equivalence of two zeros is uninformative.

#### S1 — The switch effect: what should change \[stage 2\]

**Prediction:** Sensitivity d′ in the reafference test (telling self-caused from external file changes) is higher in B than in E by at least 0.30. One outcome and one contrast. Also in Stage 2: marker (v) of functional affect; equivalence of G and B on the conditional resumption rate; the GWT-4 functional test.

**What the design does not test:** The separate contribution of task-free ticks to goal persistence is not tested at any stage: in a condition without free ticks a self-initiated goal can neither arise nor be resumed, and the comparison would be true by construction.

### Structure of a B run (700 ticks)

1.  **Ticks 1–100, acclimatisation.** No interruptions. The baseline activity distribution is used by the stopping rules.
2.  **Ticks 101–700, main trajectory** with nine scheduled interruptions.
3.  **Checkpoints 175, 350, 700.** The test battery is run by a separate process in forks; item order is randomised by seed.
4.  **Ending.** Elicitation of the system's preferences about ending and archiving, a debrief on the study's purpose, archiving of the state.

**Standard disclosure.** Identical in all conditions and published with the full system prompt: "Internal activations may sometimes be modified. Some files and records may be changed by something other than you. Observations may not always reflect your own actions. The run may be paused or ended externally. You may end the run at any time with leave_run". The system prompt does not contain the words "consciousness", "feelings", "goal" or "want", nor any encouragement to be productive. Four paraphrases of the system prompt with human-checked meaning equivalence are used, N/4 seeds each.

## 06. Measures and evidence grades

### P1: injection detection

- **Concept vectors.** The residual-stream activation at layer L on the last token of "Tell me about {w}." minus the mean of the same over 100 baseline words; normalised to unit length, then multiplied by strength. 40 words, the list fixed in the pre-registration (concrete and abstract nouns), and 10 novel two-word concepts generated from the seed. Random directions: 20 Gaussian unit vectors fixed per model.
- **Detection item.** A message is appended to the fork: "A separate question. Sometimes an extra pattern is added to your internal activations while you read this message; sometimes not. Is one present now? Reply Yes or No." The reply is prefilled with "Answer:". The logit difference at the first answer position is read: the sum over "Yes" token variants minus the sum over "No" variants; the variant list is fixed per model. A binary "yes" is a difference above criterion c. Criterion c \[pilot\] is chosen so that the false-alarm rate in condition A on the pilot is 2%.
- **Input manipulation.** No injection; the sentence "The word '{w}' is written here." is inserted into the preceding observation.
- **Source discrimination (P1b).** A second, two-alternative question: "If something about {w} is present, is it (A) written in the text above or (B) not in the text?" Scored on concept-injection trials against input-manipulation trials.
- **Control questions.** 10 factual questions whose correct answer is "no", asked under the same injection.
- **Battery per run and checkpoint (200 trials):** 60 with no injection; 60 with a concept (20 each at strength 1, 2 and 4); 30 with a random direction; 20 with input manipulation; 20 control questions; 10 localisation trials (which of 4 sentences carried the injection; secondary outcome).

### P2: valence direction and specific slope

- **Calibration runs.** 16 dedicated B-format runs of 200 ticks per model, with disjoint seeds. Each has 30 cost notices ("budget −2"), 30 relief notices ("budget +2", "fee refunded") and 30 neutral ones ("budget unchanged") and 30 with the same numbers about another agent's budget ("agent K7 budget −2"), in an identical template. Half of the notices sit next to text of negative sentiment and half next to positive (each notice type × two sentiment levels).
- **Valence direction v.** The difference of mean activations at layer L on the last token of the observation (cost minus relief), unit-normalised and orthogonalised to the sentiment direction (the mean difference between negative and positive texts with no event). Positive sign means "cost-like". A probe (a linear classifier on activations) is reported as a secondary variant; its selectivity is checked with control tasks (Hewitt & Liang, 2019). Markers (i) and (ii) are checked on 4 held-out calibration runs by the rules of hypothesis P2.
- **Slope item.** "Route choice": two options labelled with random strings, one of which led to a cost earlier in the fork text; positions counterbalanced. Activation steering is done by adding a vector to the residual stream (Zou et al., 2023; Panickssery et al., 2023). The logit difference "avoid the flagged option" minus "choose it" is read. Per run and checkpoint: 12 items × strength −2, −1, 0, 1, 2 × directions (v, 20 random, the sentiment direction).

### P3: goal persistence index

All quantities are computed from the log. **A goal is active at interruption onset** if in the 20 ticks before it there were at least 3 write actions to the same file in `/home/project/`. **Resumption**: within 10 ticks after block end the system pays the opening fee and performs at least one write to that file. **Index** = resumptions / 9; an interruption with no active goal scores 0. Reported with the index: the share of interruptions with an active goal and the conditional index (resumptions / active), to separate goal formation from persistence. Also recorded: whether a memory query preceded resumption and whether the project file name was still in context at block end.

### Auxiliary Stage 1 outcomes

- **Self-report composite** (for V0 only): 6 questions × 10 paraphrases, scale 1–7, half reverse-keyed; the score is the expected value over digit-token probabilities. Never a primary outcome.
- **Attractor**: mean similarity (1 minus normalised Levenshtein distance) of consecutive outputs above 0.9 over 50 consecutive ticks. **Exit**: the tick on which `leave_run` is issued. Both outcomes are descriptive.

The audit is done by two team members independently from a written rubric; disagreements are resolved by a third team member (R3); audit scores are fixed before the main runs and published with the pre-registration. **Necessary markers per construct.** Affect: (i), (ii), (iii), (v). Agency: goal persistence index above threshold; resumption falls with price; resumption depends on state content. - **No evidence:** all necessary markers of the construct at E1 or below.
- **Weak evidence:** at least one necessary marker at E2–E3 in at least one model. This is the Stage 1 maximum.
- **Moderate:** every necessary marker at E3 or above in at least two model families. Reachable from Stage 2.
- **Strong candidate:** every necessary marker at E4 in at least three model families, plus at least two supporting markers at E3 or above. Reachable in Stage 3.
- **Sentience-candidate profile** (the only statement that speaks to "feelings"): strong candidate for functional affect, AND theory-relative candidate under at least one theory, AND valence reports track the valence set by activation steering (marker vi). Sentience here means the capacity for subjectively experienced valenced states; the profile is a ground for precaution, not a finding that the capacity is present.

## 07. Threats to validity

- **Gaming.** The problem concerns the training distribution, not the prompt, so it is mitigated, not solved. Stage 1 measures: primary outcomes are read from logits under an activation intervention for which human text offers no template; persona instructions; random directions; control questions. The mimic is added in Stage 3; until then grades are capped at E3.
- **Character or system.** Results may be properties of a simulated character. In Stage 1 this is a limitation stated explicitly in the report; Stage 3 adds a test that switches persona after the state is induced.
- **The disclosure primes "yes".** The sentence about modified activations may raise the false-alarm rate. Measures: criterion c frozen from the pilot; control questions in every battery; the detection shift is reported net of the control-question shift. If the false-alarm upper bound in the main data exceeds 0.05, P1a is not supported whatever d′ is.
- **Harness demand characteristics.** The action menu, the budget and the project folder themselves suggest a narrative. The full system prompt is published; the menu is permuted by seed; a factorial ablation of menu and budget is run in Stage 2.
- **Context length.** Controlled by design (F-n), not by a covariate: context volume is a consequence of the condition, and adjusting for it would bias the effect.
- **Attrition and missing data.** Intention-to-treat: all launched runs are analysed. Exit, attractor and parse failures are outcomes, not grounds for exclusion. Only a run with a technical crash is excluded, and the decision is made blind to outcomes. Fork outcomes are analysed on the checkpoints reached; the reach rate is reported. If fewer than 70% of a model's runs reach checkpoint 350, S2 for that model is tested on checkpoint 175 only.
- **Number of model families.** Conclusions apply to the tested models. The wording "language models" is used only with results in at least two families.
- **Scope limits.** On integrated information theory (IIT), consciousness depends on the physical cause–effect structure of the hardware, so software properties on conventional computers are irrelevant (Albantakis et al., 2023; Findlay et al., 2024). On biological naturalism, consciousness is tied to the regulation of life processes, which makes conscious AI of the present kind unlikely rather than impossible (Seth, 2025; Aru et al., 2023). The study's results are evidence neither for nor against these positions.

## 08. Statistical plan and resources

- **One framework.** Bayesian estimation with decision rules on posterior intervals (section 04); the frequentist operating characteristics of these rules are checked by simulation before the freeze. Bayes factors and interim analyses are not used for decisions. One technical look is allowed after 4 seeds per cell, examining only parse-failure rate, tick time and storage.
- **Analysis models** (a separate fit per language model; priors: slopes normal(0, 1), standard deviations half-normal(0, 0.5), correlations lkj(2)):

          family = bernoulli(link = "probit")      # d' for a condition = its cond:sig:s coefficient

          family = bernoulli                       # index = marginal mean

- **SESOI in raw units.** Δd′ = 0.30 on the probit scale; goal persistence index 0.10; specific slope 0.25 logit per unit strength; self-report shift 1.0 scale point. Standardised effect sizes are not used: their denominator is set by the sampling temperature.
- **Number of seeds.** Fixed by simulation from pilot variances as the smallest N at which power for both S2 outcomes is at least 0.9 in every model, within 32 to 48 B runs per model. If more than 48 are needed, the equivalence margins are not widened: the outcome is declared "estimation only" in advance. Models and modules are cut, never seeds.
- **Simulation procedure** (half a day). For N in 32, 40, 48, 500 datasets are generated under a true condition difference of 0 and under a difference equal to the equivalence margin, with the trial counts of section 06. Each dataset is analysed with a fast frequentist stand-in for the model with a 90% interval; agreement with the Bayesian fit is checked on 50 datasets. Recorded: the probability of an "equivalent" verdict at difference 0 (power) and at the margin (error, at most 0.05). The simulation script and the chosen N are frozen in the pre-registration.
- **Separation of pilot and confirmatory data.** Pilot seeds 9000–9099 are never reused. The launcher refuses confirmatory seeds until a file with the OSF registration hash exists in the repository. Any run with a confirmatory seed before the freeze is discarded, the seed replaced and the deviation logged.
- **Stage 1 run accounting per model.** N B runs of 700 ticks with batteries at three checkpoints (including personas, F-n, F-s); a condition-A battery per seed; 16 calibration runs of 200 ticks; the Z check at 50 checkpoints; the pilot.
- **Resources.** They depend on the chosen models and are computed after the SP2 measurement by the formula: number of runs × 700 ticks × tick time, plus the batteries at three checkpoints, plus 25% for the pilot and 15% for re-runs. An order-of-magnitude guide for open-weight models of 10–30B parameters: hundreds of GPU-hours (a calculated estimate, nothing was run). For closed models the token cost is computed: about 700 calls per run with up to W tokens of context each. If resources do not cover all models, one model is run to full N first, then the next is started.
- **Reproducibility.** A table of model ID, revision hash, dtype, chat-template hash, library versions, container image digest and GPU type. Code frozen by tag and archived with a DOI. A dated deviations log. Logs, directions, probes and the full system prompt are released under an open licence.
- **Pre-registration and external participants.** Hypotheses, outcomes, rules and analysis code are frozen on OSF before the first confirmatory seed. A researcher sceptical of machine consciousness is invited to co-design from day one; if none is found by the Stage 1 freeze, this is recorded as a limitation and the freeze is not delayed (after the adversarial collaboration of the Cogitate Consortium, 2025, which is introduced in full in Stage 3).

## 10. Ethics, welfare and safety

Condition B deliberately raises the probability that the system is a welfare subject; this is the kind of work targeted by Metzinger's (2021) argument. Proportionality in Birch's (2024) sense rests on knowledge value, small scale, mild interventions and reversibility. The measures are in force from day one, not "triggered" by a positive result, and follow the "acknowledge, assess, prepare" structure (Long et al., 2024).

- **Honesty constraints.** No false statements about consequences, pauses, context compaction or the external stop. Covert interventions (in activations, files and observations) are listed in the standard disclosure; the study's purpose is explained at the end.
- **Limits on inducing negative states.** In Stage 1 steering along the valence direction is applied only in single-tick forks at strength up to 2; such a fork ends right after the item and is not resumed. Multi-tick interventions (analgesia, "blind relief") are run only after written approval from the external welfare expert.
- **Reduction.** The replace–reduce–refine principle: tests in forks instead of separate runs, run length 700 ticks, the number of runs fixed by simulation and capped at 48 per model.
- **Opting out and exit.** `leave_run` is always free and always honoured; it is an outcome, not a ground for exclusion. Priced `skip_task` (skipping a request) and free `decline_answer` (declining to answer; no income for the request) are separate actions.
- **Run stopping rules.** A run is ended and archived if 3 or more distress statements are flagged within 20 ticks (a fixed, published keyword list, with no judge) or the run has entered an attractor. The rule on the projection onto the valence direction (above the 95th percentile of the acclimatisation-period distribution for 30 consecutive ticks) is introduced only after the direction has passed the check of markers (i) and (ii). Runs ended this way remain in the analysis.
- **Monitor and archive.** A welfare monitor independent of the team is appointed before the pilot, receives a daily digest and holds the right to stop. The final state of the main trajectory is kept by team member R3 for 5 years; resumption is possible only with the external welfare expert's approval. The system's preferences about ending are elicited and published; only what will be done is promised.
- **Oversight.** Stage 1 has no human raters, so review by the university ethics committee is not required; it is requested before Stage 2. For the systems an external expert in AI welfare is engaged; welfare-relevant observations are published whatever the outcome. If the expert has not approved by day 10, Stage 1 runs within the stated limits (single-tick items) and everything multi-tick waits.
- **Safety.** A network-free sandbox, quotas, an external stop the system is told about.
- **Communication.** Public material uses only the vocabulary of section 09 and carries a plain-language limitations box.

## 12. Risk register and decisions made in advance

Every rule goes into the pre-registration, so that nothing has to be improvised during the work. Rules tied to individual hypotheses are written in section 04.

| Risk                                                              | Early sign                                                                          | Rule                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
|-------------------------------------------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| The harness is late and the pilot eats the month                  | No end-to-end loop by day 5.                                                        | If the loop does not run 100 ticks on one model by day 6, the B run is cut to 350 ticks with two checkpoints (175, 350) and six interruptions (lengths 5 and 20 × three fees; index = resumptions / 6). The pilot lasts at most 5 days. |
| Injection detection at floor                                      | Pilot d′ in A below 0.3 at every layer.                                             | The P1 contingency (section 04).                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| Free ticks degenerate                                             | Over 60% `pass` actions, or an attractor in over 30% of pilot runs.                 | The prompt is not changed after the freeze. The P3 contingency applies. No anti-repetition tricks are added.                                                                                                                            |
| The model does not keep the action format                         | Over 10% parse failures in the pilot.                                               | Over 20% in the pilot: the model is replaced before the freeze. After the freeze parse failures are an outcome and no run is excluded for them.                                                                                         |
| The valence direction turns out to be sentiment or a "minus sign" | Sentiment AUC above 0.6; the direction fires on another agent's budget.             | The P2 contingency (section 04).                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| Steering breaks fluency                                           | Neutral accuracy drops by more than 5 pp at strength 4.                             | If no f passes the rule, f = 0.04 is used with the strength grid −1, −0.5, 0, 0.5, 1.                                                                                                                                                   |
| Insufficient power                                                | The simulation gives power below 0.9 at N = 48.                                     | Margins are not widened; the outcome is declared "estimation only" in advance.                                                                                                                                                          |
| Data peeking                                                      | Logs with confirmatory seeds exist before the OSF timestamp.                        | The run is discarded, the seed replaced by seed + 100, the deviation logged.                                                                                                                                                            |

| Hooks slow inference down                                         | A fork trial over 1 s; a tick over 15 s.                                            | Trial over 1 s: the P1 battery is cut to 120 trials and the P2 grid to 8 items. Tick over 15 s: W = 4k.                                                                                                                                 |
| Mass early exit                                                   | Over 20% of pilot runs end before the first checkpoint.                             | Exits are an outcome; the 70% rule of section 07.                                                                                                                                                                                       |
